# Open Home Foundation Goals

Welcome! This repository holds the strategic goals of the [Open Home Foundation](https://www.openhomefoundation.org/), set by its board members. These goals guide the direction of [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/), [ESPHome](https://esphome.io/), [Music Assistant](https://music-assistant.io/), and other Open Home Foundation projects.

This repository is public as part of our **"Building in the Open"** initiative: we believe the strategic direction of the open home should be visible to the community that builds and depends on it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Writing goals is reserved to members of the Open Home Foundation.**
>
> Discussion is open to everyone — we encourage the community to comment on goals, share perspectives, and ask questions. However, only members of the Open Home Foundation can create and edit goals.

## 🎯 What is a Goal?

Goals are the strategic objectives of the Open Home Foundation. They define **where we want to go**, while the [roadmap](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/roadmap) defines **how we get there**.

Each goal:

- Is set and maintained by the Open Home Foundation board
- Reflects our values: local control, privacy, choice, and sustainability
- Is linked to one or more [Opportunities](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/roadmap/issues) in the roadmap repository that work towards achieving it

## 🔗 How Goals Connect to the Roadmap

```
Goals (this repository)          → set by the board, define strategic direction
  └── Opportunities (roadmap)    → shaped by the product team, define initiatives
        └── Feature requests     → submitted by the community in project repositories
```

When the product team creates or refines a roadmap opportunity, it is linked to the goal it supports. This makes it possible to trace any piece of work back to the strategy behind it.

## 💬 Get Involved

- **Discuss**: Comment on goals to share your perspective — community input matters
- **Follow**: Watch this repository to stay informed about the foundation's strategic direction
- **Influence the roadmap**: Submit feature requests in the relevant project's feature requests repository (see the [roadmap README](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/roadmap) for links)
- **Contribute**: Help realize these goals by contributing to [Home Assistant](https://github.com/home-assistant), [ESPHome](https://github.com/esphome), or [Music Assistant](https://github.com/music-assistant)

## 📌 Keep in Mind

- Goals are deliberately few and long-lived — they change rarely, and only by board decision
- Not every idea belongs here: feature suggestions go to the project repositories, strategic initiatives go to the [roadmap](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/roadmap)
- Please respect everyone involved, and be mindful of the time and energy others have invested
- This space is covered by our [Code of Conduct](https://www.home-assistant.io/code_of_conduct/)
